FAQ

The same answers as the Help screen inside the app.

The basics

What does Snug do?

Snug puts small app icons on your home screen in widgets, so lots of apps fit in the space of a few.

  • Each widget has its own apps, order, size and look. Make one for work, one for games, one for money.
  • Resize a widget and the icons rearrange and shrink to fit, like a 1×2 widget holding two columns of six.
  • Notification counts show on the icons, like on your home screen.

Snug is not a launcher: it works with the home screen you already have.

How do I add a widget?

Open Snug and tap Add a widget, or long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and pick Snug. The editor opens right away: start blank, copy another widget, or start from a saved layout.

  • Resize: long-press the widget on the home screen and drag its handles.
  • Edit later: tap Edit in Snug, or long-press the widget and tap the pencil ("Customize").
  • Remove: drag it off the home screen like any widget.
What can I put in a widget?
  • Apps: tick as many as you like.
  • Shortcuts: things apps offer to put on the home screen, like a WhatsApp chat, a direct-dial contact or Maps directions.
  • Links (Pro): any web address. It shows as a letter tile unless you give it an icon.
  • Sections (Pro): a title (or a plain divider) that starts a new group of icons. Sections can sit side by side as a grid: see Sections and grids.

Tap an item in the editor to rename it or give it another icon. Hold the handle to reorder the list, or hold an icon in the preview and drop it where you want it.

Why can't I add the shortcuts I see when I long-press an app?

Android only lets your home-screen app (the launcher) read those. Every widget app has this limit. Snug lists the other kind, the shortcuts apps offer to any app, and many apps offer the same things that way: chats, contacts, directions, labels, files.

Layout and look

How does the layout work?
  • Auto: icons fill whatever size the widget is. The icon size is a maximum: when apps don't fit, icons shrink until they do.
  • Fixed: you pick the rows and columns.
  • Tall widgets read down each column first; wide ones read across.
  • Scroll: when apps don't fit, keep the icons at full size and scroll inside the widget.
  • Sections: stacked one under another, or side by side as a grid, each with its own look if you like. See Sections and grids.

The editor's preview shows the widget at its real size; if some apps can't fit, it says how many.

What can I change about the look?
  • Icon style: the apps' own icons, or Themed: one colour, using the apps' monochrome icons where they have them.
  • Icon pack: use any installed icon pack (the ones made for launchers like Nova).
  • Shape: circle, squircle, rounded or teardrop, so mixed icons look uniform.
  • Labels: names under the icons, in any colour and size.
  • Section titles: any font: your phone's own, the ones that come with Snug, or any Google Fonts family, size, colour, bold and alignment, and an optional coloured badge behind the title. Emoji work too. To style one section differently, tap its title in the app list and turn on Own title style.
  • Background: transparent, solid or a rounded card, with your colour and opacity.
  • Dark mode: give the widget different colours while your phone is in dark mode.
Can I change how the Snug app itself looks?

Yes, with an app theme: menu (top left), App theme. Pick a built-in theme, Create your own theme (colours, font, text size, corners and borders), have your AI design one with AI prompt, or Import a theme from a file. App themes style Snug's own screens; your widgets keep the look you give each of them in its editor. The themes guide has the details.

Can I sort the icons?
  • Manual: exactly the order you set.
  • A–Z: alphabetical; new apps fall into place by themselves.
  • Most used: the apps you opened most in the last two weeks come first. It needs Usage access, which only lets Snug count app opens on your phone. It re-sorts every few hours.

With sections, each section is sorted on its own.

Sections and grids PRO

How do sections work?

A section is a title (or a plain divider, if you leave the title empty) with the apps under it. In the editor's app list, a section holds every app below it, down to the next section. So the list reads top to bottom, exactly like the widget.

  • Add section and Add apps both add to the end of the list. Build in reading order: a section, then its apps, then the next section, then its apps.
  • To move an app into another section, hold its handle in the list (or the icon in the preview) and drop it below that section's title.
  • Move earlier and Move later, in a section's sheet, move the whole section with its apps.
  • Up to 12 sections per widget.
How do I build a grid?

A grid is rows of sections side by side. Here's a 2 × 2 grid:

Google4 apps
Phone4 apps
Watch2 apps
Tools5 apps
  1. Tap Add section and title it Google. Close the sheet, tap Add apps and pick its apps.
  2. Add section again: Phone. In its sheet, under Place in the widget, turn on Next to the section before. It moves to the right of Google. Add its apps.
  3. Add section: Watch. Leave Next to the section before off, so it starts a new row. Add its apps.
  4. Add section: Tools, with Next to the section before on. Add its apps. Done.

To change a section later, tap its title in the app list: the same sheet opens, just below the preview, so you see each change as you make it.

How are rows and widths worked out?
  • Rows: a section with Next to the section before on joins the row of the section before it. A section with it off starts a new row. A row is as tall as its tallest section.
  • Widths: once a section shares a row, its sheet shows Width in its row, from 1 to 6. Sections split the row by these shares: 2 and 1 gives two thirds and one third.
  • Columns: there's no separate column setting. Sections line up as columns when every row has the same number of sections with the same widths, like the 2 × 2 grid above.

Is there a quicker way to arrange them?

With two or more sections, Layout has a Sections block that shows your rows. Stack all puts every section on its own row; All in one row puts them all side by side. Space between sections sets the gap between sections and between rows.

Can each section look different?
  • Its icons and look: in a section's sheet, This section's icons and look has the widget's Layout and Look settings for that section only: icon size, labels, shape, background and more. Use the widget's settings resets it.
  • Its title: turn on Own title style for its own font, size, colour, alignment and badge.
  • Its spacing: turn on Own icon spacing, then use Same across and down, or set Across and Down separately.

Each section's icon size is a maximum: a section with many apps, or a narrow share of its row, shrinks its icons until they all fit. When the whole grid is too tall for the widget, every section shrinks together. Sections in a grid can't scroll.

Are sections free?

Sections and grids are part of Pro, which is free during early access. Everyone who installs during early access keeps it.

Badges

How do notification badges work?

Badges show how many notifications an app has, as a number or a dot, in your colour, size and corner. They need Notification access. Snug only counts notifications per app: it doesn't read, keep or send what they say.

A badge shows the wrong number.

Some apps group many messages into one notification, so their count can be lower than you expect. Badges also need Notification access to stay on: if they all disappear, open Snug. While the access is off, the top of its main screen offers to turn it back on, and the menu's Permissions page shows it any time.

Backup and new phones

Will my widgets come back on a new phone?

Yes. Your widgets are part of your phone's Google backup and come back with your apps. If a widget can't be matched on the new phone, its setup is kept as a saved layout: add a widget and pick it under Start from.

You can also back up by hand: in Snug's menu (top left), Backup and restore, Back up to file, and save it to Google Drive or anywhere else. The file holds your widgets, saved layouts, custom icons and the app theme. Restore from file asks before replacing widgets that are still on your home screen; everything else comes back as saved layouts.

You can also open or share a backup or layout file to Snug to import it. Anything another app hands to Snug asks first: Import into Snug?

Build with AI

Can an AI build my widgets?

Yes. Describe what you want to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI assistant and it writes the layout. In Snug's menu (top left), tap Build with AI and Copy my setup, paste it into your AI and say what you want. It can restyle the Snug app itself too, through the app theme. Then copy its answer and tap Import from clipboard, or share the answer to Snug. A new layout is saved and you can put it on a widget right away. If the AI changed one of your widgets, Snug asks before replacing it. It is a Pro feature, free for now. The format is documented here.

Does Snug send my apps to an AI?

No. Snug sends nothing. Copy my setup puts your widgets, their sizes and your installed apps on the clipboard, and it only leaves your phone if you paste it into an AI yourself.

Pro FREE FOR NOW

What's free, and what's Pro?

Free covers 2 widgets with every basic: auto-fit layouts, ordering, drag to reorder, renaming, badges, labels, backgrounds, themed icons, shortcuts and backups. Pro adds:

  • Unlimited widgets
  • Icon packs, and any icon or image for any app
  • Icon shapes: circle, squircle, rounded, teardrop
  • Scrolling widgets for long lists
  • Links and sections, including side-by-side grids
  • A different look in dark mode
  • Most-used order
  • Build layouts with AI: describe them, your AI writes them
Is Pro really free?

During early access, yes: every Pro feature is free for everyone who installs Snug, and you keep Pro for good, even after it starts costing money. Later, Pro becomes a single payment through Google Play: no subscription and no ads, ever.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Pro is tied to your Google account. Install Snug with the same account and open it; if Pro doesn't show, tap Restore purchase on the Pro screen.
