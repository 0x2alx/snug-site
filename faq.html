The basics

What does Iconic do?

Iconic puts small app icons on your home screen in widgets, so lots of apps fit in the space of a few.

  • Each widget has its own apps, order, size and look. Make one for work, one for games, one for money.
  • Resize a widget and the icons rearrange and shrink to fit, like a 1×2 widget holding two columns of six.
  • Notification counts show on the icons, like on your home screen.

Iconic is not a launcher: it works with the home screen you already have.

How do I add a widget?

Open Iconic and tap Add a widget, or long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and pick Iconic. The editor opens right away: start blank, copy another widget, or start from a saved layout.

  • Resize: long-press the widget on the home screen and drag its handles.
  • Edit later: tap Edit in Iconic, or long-press the widget and tap the pencil ("Customize").
  • Remove: drag it off the home screen like any widget.
What can I put in a widget?
  • Apps: tick as many as you like.
  • Shortcuts: things apps offer to put on the home screen, like a WhatsApp chat, a direct-dial contact or Maps directions.
  • Links: any web address. It shows as a letter tile unless you give it an icon.
  • Sections: a title (or a plain divider) that starts a new group of icons.

Tap an item in the editor to rename it or give it another icon. Hold the handle to reorder the list, or hold an icon in the preview and drop it where you want it.

Why can't I add the shortcuts I see when I long-press an app?

Android only lets your home-screen app (the launcher) read those. Every widget app has this limit. Iconic lists the other kind, the shortcuts apps offer to any app, and many apps offer the same things that way: chats, contacts, directions, labels, files.

Layout and look

How does the layout work?
  • Auto: icons fill whatever size the widget is. The icon size is a maximum: when apps don't fit, icons shrink until they do.
  • Fixed: you pick the rows and columns.
  • Tall widgets read down each column first; wide ones read across.
  • Scroll: when apps don't fit, keep the icons at full size and scroll inside the widget.
  • Sections (up to 12): in Layout, Sections shows them in rows. Tap Stack all or All in one row, or tap a section's title and, under Place in the widget, turn on Next to the section before and set its Width in its row. Space between sections sets the gap. Several rows of side-by-side sections are Pro; stacked sections or one row are free.
  • A section's own look: in the same sheet, This section's icons and look has the widget's Layout and Look settings for that section only (icon size, labels, shape, background and more). Use the widget's settings resets it. Sections in a grid can't scroll.
  • Own icon spacing: tap a section's title in the app list, turn on Own icon spacing, and use Same across and down, or set Across and Down separately.

The editor's preview shows the widget at its real size; if some apps can't fit, it says how many.

What can I change about the look?
  • Icon style: the apps' own icons, or Themed: one colour, using the apps' monochrome icons where they have them.
  • Icon pack: use any installed icon pack (the ones made for launchers like Nova).
  • Shape: circle, squircle, rounded or teardrop, so mixed icons look uniform.
  • Labels: names under the icons, in any colour and size.
  • Section titles: size, bold, font and alignment for every section title (emoji work too). To style one section differently, tap its title in the app list.
  • Background: transparent, solid or a rounded card, with your colour and opacity.
  • Dark mode: give the widget different colours while your phone is in dark mode.
Can I sort the icons?
  • Manual: exactly the order you set.
  • A–Z: alphabetical; new apps fall into place by themselves.
  • Most used: the apps you opened most in the last two weeks come first. It needs Usage access, which only lets Iconic count app opens on your phone. It re-sorts every few hours.

With sections, each section is sorted on its own.

Badges

How do notification badges work?

Badges show how many notifications an app has, as a number or a dot, in your colour, size and corner. They need Notification access. Iconic only counts notifications per app: it doesn't read, keep or send what they say.

A badge shows the wrong number.

Some apps group many messages into one notification, so their count can be lower than you expect. Badges also need Notification access to stay on: if they all disappear, check Iconic's status on its home screen.

Backup and new phones

Will my widgets come back on a new phone?

Yes. Your widgets are part of your phone's Google backup and come back with your apps. If a widget can't be matched on the new phone, its setup is kept as a saved layout: add a widget and pick it under Start from.

You can also back up by hand: in Iconic's menu, Back up to file, and save it to Google Drive or anywhere else. Restore from file brings the setups back as saved layouts.

Build with AI

Can an AI build my widgets?

Yes. Describe what you want to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI assistant and it writes the layout. In Iconic's menu, tap Build with AI and Copy my setup, paste it into your AI and say what you want. Then copy its answer and tap Import from clipboard, or share the answer to Iconic. A new layout is saved and you can put it on a widget right away. If the AI changed one of your widgets, Iconic asks before replacing it. It is a Pro feature, free for now. The format is documented here.

Does Iconic send my apps to an AI?

No. Iconic sends nothing. Copy my setup puts your widgets, their sizes and your installed apps on the clipboard, and it only leaves your phone if you paste it into an AI yourself.

Pro FREE FOR NOW

What's free, and what's Pro?

Free covers 2 widgets with every basic: auto-fit layouts, ordering, drag to reorder, renaming, badges, labels, backgrounds, themed icons, shortcuts and backups. Pro adds:

  • Unlimited widgets
  • Icon packs, and any icon or image for any app
  • Icon shapes: circle, squircle, rounded, teardrop
  • Scrolling widgets for long lists
  • Links and sections
  • A different look in dark mode
  • Most-used order
Is Pro really free?

During early access, yes: every Pro feature is free for everyone who installs Iconic, and you keep Pro for good, even after it starts costing money. Later, Pro becomes a single payment through Google Play: no subscription and no ads, ever.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Pro is tied to your Google account. Install Iconic with the same account and open it; if Pro doesn't show, tap Restore purchase on the Pro screen.
