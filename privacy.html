Iconic Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026. Applies to the Iconic Android app (dev.alx.iconic).

The short version: Iconic has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Everything it knows stays on your phone. Nothing reaches us unless you email us.

Who we are

Iconic is made by Clumsy Clanker, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, write to 0x2alx@proton.me.

What Iconic uses on your phone

  • Your list of apps: Iconic lists the apps you can open from your home screen, so you can pick which ones go in a widget, and reads their icons and names to draw them. It also lists the shortcuts apps offer and the icon packs you have installed.
  • Notification access (optional, for badges): Iconic counts how many notifications each app has. It doesn't read, store or send what the notifications say; the counts are kept in memory only, to draw the badges.
  • Usage access (optional, only for "Most used" order): Iconic counts how often each app was opened on your phone in the last two weeks, to sort your icons. The counts are kept in memory only and never leave your phone.
  • Build with AI (optional): when you tap Copy my setup, Iconic puts your widgets, their sizes and the list of apps you can open from your home screen on the clipboard. It happens only when you tap it, and Iconic never sends it anywhere. It leaves your phone only if you paste it into another app or website yourself.
  • Images you pick (optional, for custom icons): Iconic copies the image you choose, shrunk to icon size, into its own storage. It can't see your other photos.

What Iconic stores, and where

In Iconic's private storage on your phone: your widget setups (which apps, shortcuts, links and sections each widget has, their names, order and look), saved layouts, copies of shortcut icons and of images you picked as icons, whether you bought Pro, and the date you first installed Iconic (which decides early access to Pro). Shortcuts are stored as the apps hand them over, so a shortcut to a contact or a chat includes what's needed to open it, like that contact's number. Uninstalling Iconic deletes all of it.

Android's backup to your Google account includes your widget setups, saved layouts, those icons and your first-install date, so a new phone starts set up. Google stores that backup under its own privacy policy, and you can turn backup off in Android's settings.

If you use Back up to file, Iconic writes the same information to a file where you choose (for example Google Drive). That file is yours; we never see it.

Purchases

Iconic Pro is free for early users. Once paid Pro launches, it is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; Iconic only receives confirmation that Pro was bought. We never see your name, email or payment details. Iconic's own code makes no network connections; the Google Play Billing library inside it talks to Google to process purchases.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall Iconic. That deletes everything it stored.
  • In your Google backup: the Iconic data Android backs up stays in your Google account until you delete the backup or turn backup off in Android's settings.
  • Backup files you made: delete them wherever you saved them.
  • Emails you sent us: write to 0x2alx@proton.me with the subject "Delete my Iconic data" and we delete the conversation within 30 days.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Iconic collects no personal data on our side: there are no accounts or servers, and what Iconic stores stays on your phone, under your control. The only personal data we ever receive is an email you choose to send us, which we use only to answer you. You can ask us, free of charge, to see, correct or delete it, and we answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK or a similar jurisdiction, you can also complain to your data protection authority. Our email is hosted by Proton (Switzerland).

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share any data. Apart from Google Play (purchases) and Google's backup (if you have it on), no third party is involved.

Security

Iconic's data lives in Android's app-private storage, which other apps can't read. Backup files are checked before anything in them is restored.

Children

Iconic isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. It doesn't collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
